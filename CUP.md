## 1. Introduction & Scope

This Character Usage Policy ("Policy") governs the use of all original characters created by **Levon Raupov** (also known as **Levonze**, **LevonzeFear**, **Novel Rainov**, and **Novel**) (hereinafter "Author" or "Rights Holder").

This Policy applies to:
- All currently existing characters created by the Author
- All future characters created by the Author
- Any derivative works, designs, or representations based on the Author's characters

The Author's characters are currently **private intellectual property** and are primarily intended for use in upcoming video game projects. Public disclosure and availability of these characters will occur at the Author's discretion.

This Policy is binding for any person or entity accessing, viewing, or using the Author's characters, regardless of whether this document has been explicitly acknowledged.

---

## 2. Definitions

- **"Character(s)"**: Any original character, including but not limited to visual design, personality traits, backstory, name, distinctive features, and any associated elements created by the Author.
- **"Commercial Use"**: Any use of a Character for direct or indirect financial gain, including but not limited to merchandise sales, paid commissions featuring the Character, licensing fees, advertising, or promotional purposes.
- **"Non-Commercial Use"**: Personal, fan-created works not intended for financial gain or commercial distribution.
- **"NSFW Content"**: Any sexually explicit, pornographic, or adult-oriented content, including but not limited to explicit nudity, sexual acts, or sexually suggestive material.
- **"Friendly Content"**: Content that is respectful, non-harmful, and does not promote violence, hate speech, discrimination, or illegal activities.

---

## 3. Allowed Uses

The following uses of the Author's Characters are **permitted without prior written permission**, provided they comply with all other sections of this Policy:

### 3.1 Fan Art & Derivative Artwork
- Non-commercial fan art, illustrations, and digital paintings
- Traditional artwork (drawings, paintings, sketches)

### 3.2 Edits, Collages & Memes
- Photo edits, collages, and memes featuring the Characters
- GIF animations and video edits for non-commercial purposes

### 3.3 Written Works & Media
- Mentions and appearances in fan fiction, original stories, and written works (non-commercial)
- Appearances in non-commercial comics, webcomics, and graphic novels
- Appearances in non-commercial videos, animations, and streams

**Mandatory Requirement:** All allowed uses must include clear attribution to the Author in any form (credit line, watermark, mention in description, link to Author's profile, etc.).

## 4. Restricted Uses (Require Written Permission)

The following uses require **prior written permission** from the Author before proceeding:

### 4.1 Cosplay & Physical Creations
- Cosplay costumes and props
- Handcrafted items, figurines, sculptures, and physical merchandise
- 3D prints and models

### 4.2 Character Redesigns
- Significant alterations to the Character's design, appearance, or core identity
- Re-imagined versions presented as the user's original creation

### 4.3 Commercial Use
- Any commercial application, including merchandise production, paid commissions, licensing, or promotional use

**Permission Process:**
- Requests must be submitted via the contact email provided in Section 12
- Permission is granted at the Author's discretion and may be subject to conditions
- The Author reserves the right to revoke permission if the content violates the "Friendly Content" principle or other Policy terms
- License terms, duration, and scope will be specified in writing for each approved request

## 5. Prohibited Uses

The following uses are **strictly prohibited** under all circumstances:

### 5.1 NSFW & Pornographic Content
- Creation, distribution, or publication of any sexually explicit or pornographic content featuring the Author's Characters
- **Exception:** Private, unpublished NSFW content created solely for personal use and not shared publicly or with third parties

### 5.2 NFTs & Tokenization
- Minting, selling, or distributing NFTs (Non-Fungible Tokens) featuring the Characters
- Tokenization of Character images, designs, or associated intellectual property
- Use in blockchain-based projects, cryptocurrency initiatives, or Web3 applications

### 5.3 Misrepresentation
- Claiming ownership or authorship of the Characters
- Removing, altering, or obscuring attribution or copyright notices
- Passing off derivative works as original creations unrelated to the Author's Characters
### 5.4 Special Cases
- The owner of the character reserves the right to request the removal of any work if, in his opinion, it is inappropriate or offensive

## 6. AI Generation Policy

### 6.1 Permitted AI Use
- AI-generated artwork and content featuring the Characters is allowed, **provided that**:
  - All NSFW and pornographic content restrictions (Section 5.1) are strictly observed
  - Generated content adheres to the "Friendly Content" standard
  - Proper attribution to the Author is included

### 6.2 Training Restrictions
- Using the Author's Character reference images, designs, or descriptions to train AI models for commercial purposes requires written permission
- Public AI-generated works must not be presented as the Author's official content

## 7. Discouraged Content

The following content is **strongly discouraged** but may be permitted on a case-by-case basis:

- Content depicting extreme violence, graphic gore, or torture
- Content involving political propaganda, extremist ideologies, or controversial social movements
- Content that could be considered offensive, harmful, or in poor taste

While not strictly prohibited, the Author reserves the right to request removal of such content if it is deemed to misrepresent the Character's intended portrayal or damages the Author's reputation.

**All permitted uses must include attribution to the Author in any reasonable form**, including but not limited to:
